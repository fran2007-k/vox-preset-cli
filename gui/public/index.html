Current Rig

"Play Now" makes the amp sound like a preset right now -- the same as turning its physical knobs, all at once. Nothing is written to any slot; power-cycling the amp or switching slots reverts it. Use this to just hear a preset without committing it anywhere.

Presets

"Preview" decodes the JSON with zero MIDI -- safe even with the amp off or unplugged. "Write to Amp" is the only button that touches hardware, and always asks to confirm first.

Pull a sound off the amp

Reads whatever is actually stored in a slot and saves it as a new preset file.
